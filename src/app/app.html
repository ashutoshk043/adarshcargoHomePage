About Adarsh Cargo

Building India's most reliable cargo network, one branch at a time.

Founded in 2013, CargoLine started as a single branch in New Delhi. Today we operate 46 branches, 9 hubs, and a fleet of over 1,800 vehicles moving cargo to more than 4,200 pincodes nationwide — while keeping the same operational discipline we had on day one.
